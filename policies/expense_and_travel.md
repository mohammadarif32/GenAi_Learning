# NovaTech Expense and Travel Policy

Effective date: 1 March 2026  
Applies to: Employees traveling or submitting expenses on behalf of NovaTech

## Pre-approval
- Domestic travel under INR 25,000: manager approval in the expense tool.
- Domestic travel INR 25,000 or more, and all international travel: director approval.

## Booking
- Book flights and hotels through the company travel desk.
- Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Business class only for flights of 6 hours or more with VP approval.
- Hotel cap: INR 8,000 per night in metro cities; INR 5,000 elsewhere unless pre-approved.

## Daily allowances (India)
- Meals: up to INR 1,200 per day with receipts.
- Local transport: actuals with receipts, or INR 400/day without receipts.
- Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Reimbursement
- Submit expenses within 15 days of travel or purchase.
- Claims without receipts above INR 500 will be rejected.
- Company credit card is preferred for vendor payments.

## Client entertainment
Client meals require manager approval in advance and a list of attendees. Per-person cap is INR 2,500.
