# NovaTech Code of Conduct

Effective date: 1 January 2026  
Applies to: All employees, contractors, and interns

## Purpose
This policy sets the standard of behavior expected at NovaTech. Everyone is responsible for a respectful, safe, and ethical workplace.

## Respect and inclusion
- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with dignity.
- Discrimination or harassment based on race, gender, religion, disability, age, sexual orientation, or any protected characteristic is prohibited.
- Report concerns to People Operations or ethics@novatech.example.

## Conflicts of interest
- Disclose personal, financial, or family relationships that could influence work decisions.
- Do not accept gifts worth more than INR 2,000 from vendors without written approval from your manager.

## Confidentiality
- Do not share customer data, source code, unreleased product plans, or salaries of other employees.
- Use company devices and approved tools for confidential work.

## Workplace safety
- Follow building access rules and visitor sign-in.
- Weapons, illegal drugs, and violence are strictly prohibited on company premises.

## Disciplinary action
Violations may lead to coaching, written warning, suspension, or termination, depending on severity.
