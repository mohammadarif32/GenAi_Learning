# NovaTech IT and Information Security Policy

Effective date: 15 February 2026  
Applies to: Anyone using NovaTech systems

## Accounts and passwords
- Use unique, company-managed accounts. Do not share passwords.
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters and use a password manager.
- Enable multi-factor authentication on email, VPN, GitHub, and cloud consoles.

## Acceptable use
- Company laptops, email, and Slack are for work. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not affect security or productivity.
- Do not install unapproved software.
- Do not store company files on personal Google Drive, Dropbox, or USB drives.

## Data classification
- Public: marketing website content.
- Internal: org charts, process docs.
- Confidential: customer data, source code, financials.
- Restricted: credentials, payroll, health information.

Confidential and Restricted data must stay in approved systems (Google Workspace, GitHub Enterprise, or the company CRM).

## Incident reporting
Report lost devices, phishing emails, or suspected account compromise immediately to security@novatech.example. Do not wait until the next business day for lost laptops or leaked credentials.

## Remote work
- Use the company VPN when accessing internal tools.
- Lock the screen when stepping away.
- Do not discuss confidential work in public spaces.
