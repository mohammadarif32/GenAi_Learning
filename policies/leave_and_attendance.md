# NovaTech Leave and Attendance Policy

Effective date: 1 April 2026  
Applies to: Full-time employees in India

## Working hours
- Standard hours: Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST.
- Core collaboration hours: 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM IST.
- Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

## Leave types
| Type | Annual entitlement | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Privilege Leave (PL) | 18 days | Accrues monthly. Max carry-forward: 30 days. |
| Casual Leave (CL) | 8 days | For short personal needs. Unused CL lapses at year end. |
| Sick Leave (SL) | 12 days | Medical certificate required for 3 or more consecutive days. |
| Parental Leave | 26 weeks (primary), 2 weeks (secondary) | Available after 6 months of service. |
| Bereavement Leave | 5 days | Immediate family. |

## Applying for leave
1. Submit requests in the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance (except sick leave).
2. Manager approval is required before leave starts.
3. Unapproved absence of 3 consecutive days is treated as unauthorized leave.

## Public holidays
NovaTech observes 10 declared public holidays each calendar year. The holiday list is published in December for the next year.

## Late arrival
Arriving after 10:00 AM more than 4 times in a month without approval may result in a half-day casual leave deduction.
